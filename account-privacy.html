Your account. Your learning.

What we collect

Email and password are sent over HTTPS to Supabase Auth to create and verify an account. The app never stores your password in browser storage, analytics, a learning profile or AI context. Account session tokens stay in this tab’s sessionStorage until you sign out or close the tab.

What your learning profile contains

A preferred name, subject, exam format, self-reported English level, focus skill, preferred answer method, practice duration, weekly goal and time zone. Exam date, learning goal and challenge tags are optional. We do not request legal name, date of birth, address, student ID or official grades.

Supabase stores the profile under your account ID with database policies restricting it to you. Initial signup details are also sent as private account metadata to initialise the profile. They are not public profile data. Your latest saved profile is used for practice and AI-consent decisions.

Personalisation and AI

Your subject, format and goals prefill new practice sessions. Optional AI personalisation consent is off by default and can be changed in your learning profile. No AI provider is connected in this release. A future AI action must separately explain what it sends. Its learning context excludes your email, password and authentication tokens.

Your practice data

Practice text and recordings remain on this device and are separated by signed-in account. They are not synced to Supabase by this release. Signing out hides that account’s practice in the interface; people with access to the same browser profile may still access local browser data. It is not device encryption.

Previous unassigned practice is kept under the original storage keys. It is not silently assigned to a newly created account. You can explicitly copy it into your signed-in account from Settings on a device you trust. Text exports exclude audio; download recordings separately.

Control and deletion

Edit learning preferences and revoke optional AI consent from your profile. Export or delete this account’s local practice data in Settings. Deleting local practice does not delete your Supabase account or saved learning profile. For complete account removal, contact the person who invited you to JKSpeak; the project owner can delete the account and its profile. Do not post passwords or private learning data in public issues.

Account/profile records stay until removed by the project owner; local practice stays until you delete it or the browser removes it. No analytics, advertising or automatic AI uploads are added by this account flow.
