What works

University oral exam, Viva / oral defence and presentation Q&A preparation. Add text-based PDFs, TXT or Markdown, notes, your own rubric and custom questions. Format starter questions are authored prompts, not questions generated from your materials.

Guided practice includes optional hints and notes; mock practice hides them. Both support a session timer, pause, typing, optional dictation and local recording. Interrupted sessions resume paused. This is independent rehearsal, not a proctored or certified exam.

Complete a session, check your actual answer text, and attach exact quotations to your own skill reviews. Retry individual answers with an original-answer comparison, drill flagged or low-marked questions, or transfer a skill to a different authored question. Progress shows self-reported observations and confidence separately from human examiner marks. Suggestions use explicit, evidence-linked practice flags from your last ten sessions; they are not diagnoses.

What is not connected

No AI examiner, generated follow-up conversation, automatic grading, pronunciation analysis, official IELTS/TOEFL/PTE engine, remote examiner or practice-data cloud sync. Accounts and learning profiles use Supabase when configured. No automatic score or learning gain is inferred from answer length, keywords or practice counts. Examiner marks are entered by a person on the same device and are not official grades.

Voice depends on your browser

Browser dictation may send audio to your browser vendor’s service. The app asks before starting it; check and edit the transcript. Guided practice can append a fixed skill challenge quoting your answer; this is not AI analysis. A configurable seven-day session goal tracks activity and recorded review/retry/transfer actions, not ability. Optional recording needs a supported browser, microphone permission and localhost or HTTPS. Recording does not transcribe audio. If the microphone fails, keep typing. Text-based PDFs work locally; scanned PDFs need OCR elsewhere.

Where your data lives

Account details and learning preferences are stored in Supabase when configured. Read account data information. Notes, transcripts, sessions, reviews and settings stay in this browser’s localStorage, separated by signed-in account. Audio stays in its IndexedDB. Neither is uploaded by this app. People sharing this browser profile can see the same practice records. Browser quota, private browsing, device loss and clearing site data can remove records.

Recordings can be played, downloaded or deleted from each answer review. Text exports omit audio; download recordings separately. Settings exports all text data, including unfinished practice, or deletes all sessions, notes, settings and local audio after confirmation. Data remains until you delete it or the browser removes it; there is no automatic retention schedule or cloud backup.

How to interpret progress

Exact quotation checks establish that a passage exists in the saved answer. They do not establish that your interpretation or an examiner’s judgment is correct. Self-reported strengths, practice flags, uncertainty and confidence remain labelled as such. Different exam rubrics are not combined into one ability score. Human microphone quality, transcription accuracy and Safari/Firefox behaviour require separate testing.
